# Submission summary

KarSaathi is an independent ITR-readiness guide for a first-time salaried taxpayer who has Form 16, AIS, Form 26AS, and bank-interest records but does not know how they fit together.

The prototype starts instantly with Rahul Sharma, a clearly fictional sample. A short, mobile-first journey explains each record, asks only high-signal questions, and compares the same income across sources. Rahul’s seeded missing-interest and possible-duplicate issues are not silently “fixed”: the citizen reviews them, records decisions, and sees the Tax Health result update. KarSaathi then gives an explainable, non-binding ITR-form indication, a tightly scoped visual old/new-regime comparison, and a checklist for continuing on the official e-filing portal.

This is better than recreating a large portal because it concentrates on one stressful pre-filing problem and presents one obvious action at a time. No login, upload, PAN, Aadhaar, OTP, payment, or real taxpayer information is requested. All records and backend behaviour are synthetic; there is no government-system connection, filing, verification, payment, or official status. Complex answers stop the simple recommendation and point toward qualified help.

Codex was meaningfully used to audit and re-scope the inherited repository, research official sources, implement and test deterministic domain logic, refactor the responsive accessible experience, identify failure states, and prepare submission documentation. KarSaathi is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Income Tax Department or the Government of India.
