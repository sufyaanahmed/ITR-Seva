# Submission summary

KarSaathi is an independent return-readiness guide for people who have tax records but do not know what agrees, what needs attention, or what to do next.

The feedback-driven prototype offers three clearly fictional samples: Rahul, a salaried individual; Aster Components, a domestic private company; and Mehta & Rao Advisory, an LLP. Each follows the same mobile-first five steps: understand records, compare or review the record pack, answer a few relevant questions, review a non-binding likely return route, and leave with a Tax Health checklist. Individual differences are surfaced for a decision, while entity items can be marked ready, needs attention, or uncertain—never silently “fixed.” The individual path includes a tightly scoped old/new-regime illustration. Company and firm/LLP tax calculations are intentionally omitted because a generic entity estimate would create false precision.

The firm/LLP path is one bounded non-company example, not guidance for every HUF, trust, society, AOP, BOI, or other entity. No login, upload, PAN, Aadhaar, OTP, payment, or real taxpayer information is requested. All records and behaviour are synthetic; there is no government-system connection, filing, verification, payment, or official status. Complex or uncertain answers stop the simple recommendation and point toward qualified help.

Codex was meaningfully used to audit and re-scope the inherited repository, research official sources, implement deterministic profile rules and isolated state, improve the responsive experience, identify failure states, and validate all three local journeys. KarSaathi is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Income Tax Department or Government of India.
