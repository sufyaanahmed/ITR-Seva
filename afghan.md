## How to Apply for an Indian Visa for Afghan Nationals

If you are an **Afghan national planning to travel to India**, the application process is different from the standard Indian e-Visa process used by nationals of eligible countries.

According to the Government of India's official visa portal, Afghan nationals should use the dedicated **Afghan Visa portal** rather than the standard e-Visa application system.

### Visa Categories Available for Afghan Nationals

Afghan nationals can currently apply online for the following visa categories:

- **Business Visa**
    
- **Student Visa**
    
- **Medical Visa**
    
- **Medical Attendant Visa**
    
- **Entry Visa**
    
- **UN Diplomat Visa**
    

The appropriate category depends on your purpose of travel.

### Before You Apply

Prepare the documents required for your particular visa category.

A **copy of the passport page containing your personal particulars** and a **National ID Card (Tazkira)** are required for the listed Afghan visa categories. Additional documents depend on the purpose of your visit.

You should also keep any supporting documents relevant to your application ready before starting the online form.

### Business Visa

Afghan nationals applying for a Business Visa may need:

- Passport personal-details page
    
- National ID Card (Tazkira)
    
- Invitation letter from an Indian company
    
- Original signed letter from the company in the country of residence
    
- Recommendation letter from the Afghan Chamber of Commerce or an Indian Chamber of Commerce
    
- Proof of residence in Afghanistan, if available
    
- Proof of occupation, if available
    

The exact requirements can vary depending on the business activity and visa subtype.

### Student Visa

For higher education and other eligible studies, applicants may need:

- Passport personal-details page
    
- National ID Card (Tazkira)
    
- Letter of admission from a recognized Indian educational institution
    
- Documentation showing financial support
    
- Student undertaking form
    
- Scholarship documentation, where applicable
    
- Ministry of Health and Family Welfare approval or NOC for certain medical or paramedical courses
    

The visa validity is generally linked to the duration of the educational course, subject to the applicable visa conditions.

### Medical Visa

Afghan nationals traveling to India for medical treatment may apply for a Medical Visa.

The required documents include:

- Passport personal-details page
    
- National ID Card (Tazkira)
    
- System-generated medical invitation letter from an Indian hospital
    
- Consent letter from a parent for a minor applicant
    

The official Afghan Visa portal currently lists a **Triple Entry, 2-month** Medical Visa for eligible Afghan patients.

### Medical Attendant Visa

A Medical Attendant Visa is available specifically for **Afghan nationals accompanying a patient** traveling to India for medical treatment.

The required documents include:

- Passport personal-details page
    
- National ID Card (Tazkira)
    
- System-generated medical invitation letter from the Indian hospital
    
- Any applicable supporting documents
    

The listed validity is **2 months**, with the visa validity linked to that of the principal patient's visa.

### Entry Visa

The Entry Visa category covers several specific situations for Afghan nationals.

These include certain:

- Afghan artists, cultural personalities, academics, and professionals attending qualifying cultural activities
    
- Minor children accompanying Afghan parents receiving medical treatment
    
- Children of Afghan nationals married to Indian citizens, Persons of Indian Origin, or OCI cardholders
    
- Afghan nationals who own qualifying property in India
    
- Dependents of diplomats or officials assigned to India
    
- Parents or guardians of Afghan minors studying in India
    
- Dependent parents of Afghan students
    
- Certain Afghan seamen
    
- Persons of Indian Origin without an OCI card and eligible family members
    
- Members of minority communities in Afghanistan, including Hindus and Sikhs, visiting friends or relatives or traveling for pilgrimage or religious purposes
    

The documents and validity vary according to the specific Entry Visa category.

### Completing the Online Application

Each online application form is intended for **one person**, so a separate application must be submitted for each applicant.

Enter information exactly as requested, particularly:

- Full name
    
- Passport number
    
- Date of birth
    
- Address
    
- Other personal details
    

The Government of India advises applicants to have all required documents ready before beginning the application. Documents such as invitation letters and business cards should be in English; otherwise, the application may be liable for rejection.

### After Submitting the Application

You can use the official portal to:

- Complete an application
    
- Continue a partially completed application
    
- Print an application
    
- Check visa status
    
- Re-upload documents when requested
    

If additional documents or corrections are required, follow the instructions provided through the official portal.

### Before Traveling to India

Before traveling, make sure your visa status shows **"GRANTED"** on the official website.

You should carry a copy of your Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA), where applicable, when traveling to India.

You must travel using the passport on which the visa was issued. If you receive a new passport after the ETA was issued on your old passport, the Government of India states that you should carry the old passport as well.

Biometric details are also captured at immigration upon arrival in India.

### Important: e-Arrival Card

The **e-Arrival Card is not a visa**.

Foreign nationals and OCI cardholders can complete India's e-Arrival Card online within **72 hours before arrival**. It provides arrival information to Indian immigration and is separate from the visa application.

### Official Website

Use only the Government of India's official visa portal for your application. The official portal specifically provides a separate section for **"Visa for Afghanistan"** and the available Afghan visa categories.
