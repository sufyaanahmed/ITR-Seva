## How to Apply for an Indian e-Visa

To apply for an Indian e-Visa, visit the official website.

The Indian e-Visa, also known as an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA), has nine subcategories. You can check the country-specific visa fees using the links provided in the description.

Applicants can apply online at least **4 days before their intended date of arrival**.

### Documents Required

Before applying, prepare the following documents for upload:

- A recently taken front-facing photograph with a white or light-colored background, in **JPEG format**.
    
- The passport data page, in **PDF format**.
    
- Any additional documents required for your specific visa category.
    

### Starting the Application

Enter the following information:

- Nationality
    
- Passport type
    
- Port of arrival
    
- Date of birth
    
- Email address
    
- Visa category
    
- Expected date of arrival
    

Visa notifications will be sent to the email address you provide.

Make sure to note down your **Application ID**. Click **Save and Continue**, as the website may become unresponsive or fail to load.

### Personal Details

Enter your surname and given name exactly as they appear in your passport.

Then provide:

- Details of any previous name changes
    
- Gender
    
- City and country of birth
    
- National ID number — enter **NA** if not applicable
    
- Religion
    
- Visible identification marks, such as moles, birthmarks, tattoos, or scars — enter **NA** if not applicable
    
- Educational qualification
    
- Whether your nationality was acquired by birth or naturalization
    
- Whether you have lived in the country where you are applying for at least two years
    

**Naturalization** means becoming a citizen of a country later through an application process.

### Passport and Address Details

Enter your passport details and indicate whether you have any other passports or identity certificates.

Provide your current residential address and contact number. If your permanent address is the same as your present address, select the appropriate option.

You will also need to provide your parents' details, including:

- Name
    
- Nationality
    
- Previous nationality, if applicable
    
- Place and country of birth
    

Select your marital status and provide your spouse's details, if applicable.

You must also indicate whether your parents or grandparents were Pakistani nationals or belonged to an area held by Pakistan. If yes, provide the required details.

### Employment Details

Select your occupation. If you select **Other**, specify your occupation.

Depending on your circumstances, provide:

- Employer name
    
- Designation, if applicable
    
- Employer address
    
- Phone number, if applicable
    
- Details if you are employed by a military, police, or security organization
    

### Travel Details

List all the places you intend to visit in India.

Indicate whether you booked accommodation through a tour operator and provide the relevant details if applicable.

Select your intended exit port.

You will also be asked:

- Whether you have visited India before
    
- Whether you have ever been denied permission to visit India or extend your stay
    
- Which countries you have visited during the last 10 years
    
- Whether you have traveled to any SAARC member country, other than your own, during the last 3 years
    

### References and Contact Details

For your reference in India, you can provide the contact details of a relative or friend.

If you do not have a contact in India, you may provide your hotel's information instead.

You will also need to provide contact details in your home country.

Answer the security and eligibility questions and provide additional information whenever you answer **Yes**.

### Uploading Documents

Upload your photograph in **JPEG format**. Use the cropping tool to resize and align your head within the marked area.

Upload your passport data page in **PDF format**.

Before submitting, carefully verify all the information you entered in the application.

### Visa Fee and Processing

Malaysian nationals are granted a **30-day, double-entry e-Tourist Visa on a gratis basis**, meaning no visa fee is charged.

For applicants who are required to pay a fee, the website will prompt you to make the payment, and a confirmation will be sent to your email.

Processing typically takes **3–5 business days**, although it may take longer.

You will receive updates about your application by email.

### Downloading Your e-Visa

To download your e-Visa, return to the official website and select **Check Visa Status**.

Enter your **Application ID** and **passport number**, then select **Print Status**.

Carry a copy of your ETA when you arrive in India. A digital copy is accepted, but carrying a printed copy is recommended.
